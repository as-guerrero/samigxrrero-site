Samí Guerrero

I’m a Cuban-born multidisciplinary artist based in Miami, Florida.An observer of the natural world, a healer, and a powerful manifestor.I create because it’s a need, like food and water.

My work comes from a deep reverence for the human experience and the way it’s bound to the natural elements.I work across photography, video and design.I photograph people, places and things, and have since I was young, in both analog and digital.Today I work primarily in digital video, with a strong foundation in directing and editing.

I’m drawn to the simplicity of life and to the quiet motivations behind what people do.Through overlays and experimental abstraction, I let the mundane speak louder than words ever could.

Most recently I’ve directed music videos in Miami and completed my first short film, Luz Prestada, my most important work to date.Filmed in Oriente, Cuba, it’s an experimental documentary and a personal observation of my homeland, and of the immeasurable distance between me and my loved ones.The title means “borrowed light.”In the pueblos of rural Cuba the power goes out so often that people have had to borrow light from one another, and in many places they still do.Inspired by the resilience of the Cuban spirit, the film moves through poverty, faith, love, social constructs and political thought, and through the humanity that holds them together.

All of my work is self-published.I am indebted to the sublime for everything I make.
