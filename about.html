Samí Guerrero

I’m a Cuban-born multidisciplinary artist based in Miami, Florida.An observer of the natural world and the human experience.I create because it’s a need, like food and water.

My work is rooted in a deep reverence for life, memory, identity, and our connection to the natural elements.Working across film, photography, music, and visual art, I use different mediums to explore the things that make us human.
