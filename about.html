Samí Guerrero

I’m a Cuban-born, multidisciplinary artist based in Miami, Florida: an observer of the natural world, a healer, and a manifestor. I create because I have to, the way we need food and water.

My work comes from a deep reverence for the human experience and the way it’s bound to the natural elements. I work across photography, video and design. I’ve photographed people, places and things since I was young, in both analog and digital. Today I work primarily in digital video, with a strong foundation in directing and editing.

I’m drawn to the simplicity of life and to the quiet motivations behind what people do. Through overlays and experimental abstraction, I let the mundane speak louder than words ever could.

Most recently I’ve directed music videos in Miami and completed my first short film, Luz Prestada, my most important work to date. The title means “borrowed light.” In the pueblos of rural Cuba the power goes out so often that people have had to borrow light from one another, and in many places they still do. The film is an experimental documentary about that life, seen through the eyes of a native who no longer lives there. It moves through poverty, faith, love, social constructs and political thought, and through the humanity that holds them together. Rural Cuba has been seen before, but never in this light. I made it to return to a pueblo and to hold onto its life and beauty before it slips away.

All of my work is self-published. I am indebted to the sublime for everything I make.
